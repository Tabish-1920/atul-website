Frequently Asked Questions

What Taj Mahal tour packages are available?

There are nine: same-day car, Gatimaan Express train, sunrise, Fatehpur Sikri and Mathura Vrindavan tours, plus two-day overnight tours. Departures are from Delhi and Jaipur.

Is there a Taj Mahal tour from Delhi or Jaipur?

Yes, both. Delhi has car, train, sunrise, Fatehpur Sikri, Mathura Vrindavan and overnight options. Jaipur has a full-day heritage tour and a sunrise tour, both one day.

Can I do a same day Taj Mahal tour?

Yes. The car tour and Gatimaan Express tour from Delhi are planned same-day trips covering the Taj Mahal and Agra Fort. Jaipur day trips are also available.

Is the Gatimaan Express included in a tour?

Yes. The Gatimaan Express Taj Mahal Tour from Delhi travels by train to Agra for a one-day visit to the Taj Mahal and Agra Fort.

Can I visit Fatehpur Sikri on a Taj Mahal tour?

Yes. It is part of the one-day Fatehpur Sikri tour from Delhi, the two-day Agra Heritage Tour and the two-day overnight tour from Delhi.
